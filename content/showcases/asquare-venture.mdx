Asquare Venture needed something a template built for salons and clinics
doesn't usually have to answer: multiple membership plans and a way for
someone to book a single day pass without a phone call. The same
Forge build process — Google Business Profile in, live site out — was
adapted with a plan picker and a direct WhatsApp line for day-pass
enquiries.
