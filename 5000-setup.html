Questions, answered honestly

The questions you'd actually ask.

What if I don't have a Google Business Profile yet?
No problem. We'll help you claim it for free as part of the build — WhatsApp us your business name and city, we'll walk you through it before we start the site. The whole thing takes about 20 minutes.
What if I don't like the website after you build it?
You walk away. We don't charge you. The build is the demo. You're not committing to anything by sending your GBP link — we're committing the work. If you say no, the site is taken down and your GBP is unchanged.
How can you build a real site in 45 minutes?
The site uses a template already designed for your industry (salon, clinic, café, service, studio, coach). Your photos, hours, services, and reviews come straight from your Google Business Profile — we don't write copy or design layouts from scratch. The 45 minutes is configuration + testing, not invention.
What if my business doesn't fit one of the templates?
The template gets you 80% of the way there for most local businesses. For the other 20%, we'll tell you on the call and offer the ₹15,000 Custom Site upgrade instead. You won't be charged the ₹5,000 if the template isn't right for you.
Why ₹5,000 and not ₹9,999 or ₹24,999 like the main site shows?
Those tiers include revisions, custom design, and longer delivery. The ₹5,000 setup is a simpler product — template-based, no revisions, see-before-you-pay. It's a different offer for a different buying pattern. If you need the heavier tier, see the upgrade ladder.
Who owns the domain, the email, the Cloudflare account — everything?
You do. Always. The domain is registered in your name (not Forge's). The Cloudflare account is created with your email and the password is shared with you. The email is set up on your domain. You can take everything with you if you ever stop using Forge.
What happens after the 14-day bug support ends?
Nothing breaks on its own — the template is solid. If you want changes, updates, or new content, you can either (a) do them yourself with the tools we hand over, or (b) subscribe to the ₹1,999/mo Operator plan and we handle it. There's no obligation to stay with us.
Is the ₹5,000 really all I pay?
Yes — one-time, no subscription. Domain costs (₹600-₹900/year for a .com or .in) are paid to the registrar directly, not to Forge. Cloudflare's free tier covers hosting. Email is part of the setup. No hidden monthly fees unless you opt into Operator plan later.
